Stand-in for your site’s User Profile and Reminders pages. The watch only syncs these details, so nothing is typed on the wrist.

Simulation console

Simulates data that reaches the watch through your phone and Health apps. Glucose and blood pressure need Clinical Care (Tier 3). Heart rate needs Tier 2 or above.

On a real watch: CGM and BP data come through your phone’s CGM, cuff and Health apps (often delayed, or spot readings), so the watch cannot pair sensors itself. Stress is estimated from heart-rate variability. The crown scrolls and back is a swipe.

Allergy and region filters apply on every plan. Food availability and nutrition values are estimates; check labels and ask your clinician.
